About the Aura Score & Interoceptive Energy Zones

Interoceptive Psychology & Somatic Energy Frameworks

Modern cognitive neuroscience and body-oriented psychology (such as Antonio Damasio's Somatic Marker Hypothesis) recognize that emotional vitality is directly anchored in physical bodily sensations. In contemplative traditions, these interoceptive dimensions are mapped across 7 subtle energy centers (chakras), representing foundational states ranging from psychological safety to transcendent mindfulness.

Self-Awareness Boundary: This self-reflection tool is designed for educational mindfulness and personal growth. It does not measure physical biophotons, provide medical diagnoses, or replace professional psychological healthcare.

Seven Interoceptive Energy Zones

  • Crown (Sahasrara): Transcendent perspective, existential meaning, and contemplative mindfulness.
  • Third Eye (Ajna): Intuitive pattern recognition, deep imagination, and empathic perception.
  • Throat (Vishuddha): Authentic personal expression, interpersonal assertiveness, and communicative clarity.
  • Heart (Anahata): Compassionate connection, emotional forgiveness, and social-vagal receptivity.
  • Solar Plexus (Manipura): Personal agency, proactive willpower, and resilient self-confidence.
  • Sacral (Svadhisthana): Creative inspiration, affective adaptability, and sensory joy in daily living.
  • Root (Muladhara): Somatic grounding, foundational safety, and stress stability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What psychological and contemplative traditions inspire the Aura Score Test?

A: The test blends Eastern 7-chakra somatic centers with modern interoceptive psychology (somatic markers, self-efficacy, and vagal empathy) to help users reflect on emotional vitality and psychological balance across distinct life areas.

Q: Is the aura field scan an actual biometric or scientific measurement?

A: No. The interactive silhouette and color aura scores are educational and metaphorical prompts for mindful self-reflection. They do not measure physical electromagnetic fields or serve as clinical medical diagnostics.

Q: Are my chakra responses and aura scores stored on external servers?

A: Never. All 14 chakra self-report answers, zone calculations, and visual field renderings are executed 100% locally within your device browser. No personal data is transmitted externally.
